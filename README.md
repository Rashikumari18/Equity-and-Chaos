# EQUITY AND CHAOS
 A Startup Simulation Game built with Python

"EQUITY AND CHAOS" is an interactive startup simulation game where you take the role of a founder and make important business decisions.

Every decision can affect your company's **money, customers, revenue and reputation**.

Will your startup survive the chaos? 📈

## About The Game

In EQUITY AND CHAOS, you start your own virtual company and try to grow it over a series of business events and decisions.

You choose a business type, face different situations and make decisions that influence the future of your startup.

There is no single path to success — your decisions shape the final outcome.

## Choose Your Startup

You can choose from different business types:

- 💻 Technology
- 🌱 Sustainability
- 🍔 Food & Delivery
- 🎮 Gaming

Each startup begins with its own journey and challenges.

# How To Play

1. Enter your founder name.
2. Choose your startup/business type.
3. Start your startup journey.
4. Read the business events carefully.
5. Choose from the available decisions.
6. Watch how your decisions affect your company.
7. Manage your resources and reputation.
8. Survive the challenges.
9. Complete the game and see your final score.

# Game Stats
Your startup is evaluated using important business statistics:

 -Money — Your available funds
 -Customers— Number of customers
 -Revenue — Money generated by your business
 -Reputation — How your company is perceived

Your decisions can increase or decrease these stats.
